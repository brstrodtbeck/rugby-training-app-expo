# iOS App Store Listing - Complete Checklist

## What You Need for iOS App Store

### Required Items

- [ ] **Screenshots** (at least 3, up to 10)
  - Required for iPhone (various sizes)
  - Optional for iPad
  - Recommended sizes:
    - 6.7" display: 1290 x 2796px
    - 6.5" display: 1242 x 2688px
    - 5.5" display: 1242 x 2208px

- [ ] **App Description** (up to 4000 characters)
  - What your app does
  - Key features
  - Who it's for

- [ ] **Short Description** (subtitle, up to 30 characters)
  - Brief tagline
  - Shown under app name

- [ ] **Keywords** (up to 100 characters)
  - Search terms (comma-separated)
  - Help people find your app

- [ ] **Support URL** (required)
  - Website or support page
  - Must be HTTPS

- [ ] **Privacy Policy URL** (required)
  - ✅ You already have this!

- [ ] **Age Rating Questionnaire**
  - Answer questions about content
  - Apple assigns rating

- [ ] **App Icon** (already done - 1024x1024px)

---

## Optional but Recommended

- [ ] **App Preview Video** (optional)
  - Short video showing app in action
  - Not required but helps

- [ ] **Marketing URL** (optional)
  - Website for marketing
  - Not required

- [ ] **iPad Screenshots** (optional)
  - If you want to show iPad version
