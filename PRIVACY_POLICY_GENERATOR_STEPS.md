# Privacy Policy Generator - Step-by-Step Guide

## Let's Use a Free Generator!

We'll use https://www.freeprivacypolicy.com/ - it's free and provides hosting.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Go to the Generator

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: **https://www.freeprivacypolicy.com/**
3. Click **"Start Free Privacy Policy Generator"** or **"Create Privacy Policy"**

### Step 2: Fill in Basic Information

**App/Website Information:**
- **App/Website Name:** `Progressive Overlord`
- **App/Website URL:** (You can leave blank or use a placeholder like `https://progressiveoverlord.app`)
- **Email:** `brstrodtbeck@gmail.com`
- **Country:** United States (or your country)

### Step 3: Data Collection Questions

The generator will ask what data you collect. Based on your app, here's what to select:

**Select:**
- ✅ **Personal Information** (for workout data)
- ✅ **Usage Data** (for tracking workouts/sessions)
- ✅ **Health Information** (since you use HealthKit permissions)

**Do NOT select:**
- ❌ Payment Information (your app is free)
- ❌ Location Data (you don't collect this)
- ❌ Cookies/Tracking (you don't use these)

### Step 4: Third-Party Services

**Question:** "Do you use third-party services?"

**Answer: NO** (or leave unchecked)

Your app doesn't use:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Payment processors
- Social media integrations

### Step 5: Data Storage

**Question:** "Where is data stored?"

**Answer:** "Locally on user's device" or "Device storage only"

### Step 6: Generate Policy

1. Click **"Generate Privacy Policy"** or **"Create Policy"**
2. Review the generated policy
3. Make sure it mentions:
   - Local data storage
   - No third-party services
   - Optional permissions (HealthKit, Camera, Photos)

### Step 7: Get Your URL

**Option A: Free Hosting (If Available)**
- Some generators offer free hosting
- You'll get a URL like: `https://www.freeprivacypolicy.com/live/[your-id]`
- Copy this URL - this is what you'll use in store listings!

**Option B: Download and Host Yourself**
- Download the HTML file
- Host on GitHub Pages (I can help with this)
- Or use your website

### Step 8: Save Your Privacy Policy URL

**Important:** Copy the URL you get - you'll need it for both:
- Google Play Store listing
- iOS App Store listing
