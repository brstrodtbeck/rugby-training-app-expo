# Privacy Policy for Progressive Overlord

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

## Introduction

Progressive Overlord ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Progressive Overlord stores the following information locally on your device:

- **Workout Data:** Exercise logs, sets, reps, weights, and training sessions
- **Progress Tracking:** Workout history, progression states, and performance metrics
- **Readiness Scores:** Self-reported readiness assessments for workout adjustments
- **One-Rep Maximum (1RM) Values:** Your maximum strength values for exercises
- **Session Data:** Completed workouts, set logs, and training schedules
- **Program Data:** Selected training programs and customization preferences

**Important:** All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not upload, transmit, or access this data from our servers.

### Optional Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

- **HealthKit (iOS):** To read and write health data if you choose to integrate with Apple Health
- **Camera:** For exercise form analysis (if you choose to use this feature)
- **Photo Library:** To save workout progress images (if you choose to use this feature)

These permissions are optional and only used if you grant them.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Personalization:** To customize your training programs based on your progress
- **Progress Tracking:** To track your workout history and performance over time
- **Workout Adjustments:** To adjust workout intensity based on your readiness scores
- **App Functionality:** To provide core features of the fitness tracking app

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your device
- Data is not transmitted to external servers
- Data is not shared with third parties
- We do not collect analytics or tracking data
- Your data remains private and under your control

## Third-Party Services

Progressive Overlord does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app operates independently and stores all data locally.

## Your Rights

- **Access:** You can access all your data within the app
- **Deletion:** You can delete your data by uninstalling the app (this permanently removes all stored data)
- **Control:** You control what permissions to grant (HealthKit, Camera, Photos)

## Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device until you:
- Delete the app (which removes all data)
- Clear the app's data through device settings
- Manually delete data within the app

## Children's Privacy

Progressive Overlord is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
