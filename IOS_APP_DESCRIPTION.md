## Full Description (Up to 4000 characters)

```
Progressive Overlord

The Ultimate Fitness Tracking App for Serious Athletes

Progressive Overlord is a comprehensive strength and conditioning app designed for athletes, trainers, and fitness enthusiasts who demand precision in their training. Built with advanced progression logic and readiness-based adjustments, this app adapts to your energy levels to optimize every workout.

KEY FEATURES:

📊 Smart Progression System
• Rule of 3 progression logic automatically increases weights when you're ready
• Quality gates ensure proper form and technique before progression
• Intelligent deload protocols prevent overtraining

💪 Readiness-Based Adjustments
• Rate your readiness (1-10) before each session
• Workouts automatically adjust based on your energy levels
• High readiness? Increase weight and add sets for maximum gains
• Low readiness? Reduce load for recovery and sustainability

🏋️ Comprehensive Training Programs
• 20-Week Foundation to Peak program for complete athletic development
• Rugby Season Prep: 16-week rugby-specific training
• 12-Week Strength Program for progressive strength building
• 4-Week Hypertrophy Month for muscle growth
• Recovery workouts for active rest days

📈 Advanced Progress Tracking
• Track sets, reps, weights, and RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)
• Visual progress charts and analytics
• Exercise history and performance trends
• 1RM tracking and automatic calculations
• Workout completion tracking

🎯 Personalized Workouts
• Multiple training programs to choose from
• Adjustable session schedules
• Warm-up and cool-down protocols
• Rest timer for optimal recovery between sets

📱 Intuitive Interface
• Clean, easy-to-navigate design
• Quick session access and tracking
• Calendar view for workout planning
• Exercise history and performance metrics

🔒 Privacy First
• All data stored locally on your device
• No cloud sync, no data sharing
• Your workout data stays private and secure

WHO IT'S FOR:

• Athletes seeking structured, progressive training programs
• Strength training enthusiasts wanting intelligent progression
• Rugby players and team sport athletes
• Fitness enthusiasts looking for evidence-based training
• Anyone serious about tracking and improving their performance

WHY PROGRESSIVE OVERLORD:

Unlike generic fitness apps, Progressive Overlord uses advanced progression algorithms based on training science. The Rule of 3 system ensures you progress when you're ready, not on an arbitrary schedule. Readiness-based adjustments mean your workouts adapt to how you feel, preventing injury and maximizing results.

Perfect for athletes who understand that sustainable progress comes from intelligent programming, not just harder workouts.

Start your journey to peak performance today with Progressive Overlord.

Features subject to future updates. All workout data stored locally on device.
```
