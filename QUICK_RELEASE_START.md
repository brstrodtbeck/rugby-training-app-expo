# Quick Release Start - What to Do First

## 🎯 Priority: Get Privacy Policy (REQUIRED!)

Before you can submit to either store, you **MUST** have a privacy policy URL.

### Fastest Way (15 minutes):

1. **Go to:** https://www.freeprivacypolicy.com/
2. **Fill in:**
   - App name: "Progressive Overlord"
   - What data you collect: "Workout and fitness data stored locally on device"
   - Contact email: Your email
3. **Generate** the policy
4. **Host it:**
   - Option A: GitHub Pages (free)
   - Option B: Your website
   - Option C: Use the generator's hosting (some offer free hosting)
5. **Get your HTTPS URL**

### Then Take Screenshots

While on your emulator/device:
- Take 3-8 screenshots of your app
- Show key features
- Save them for store listings

---

## Next Steps After Privacy Policy

1. ✅ Privacy policy URL ready
2. 📸 Take screenshots
3. 📝 Write app descriptions
4. 🚀 Submit to stores!

---

**Start with the privacy policy - it's required and fastest to complete!**
