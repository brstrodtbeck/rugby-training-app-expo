# Google Play Console Verification - Get Past the Block

## The Problem

Google Play Console is blocking you from proceeding until you verify your phone number and confirm you have an Android device.

## Solution: Complete the Verification

Since you can't skip it, let's complete it:

### Step 1: Verify Phone Number

1. **Enter your phone number** (any phone number works - doesn't have to be Android)
2. **Receive verification code** via SMS
3. **Enter the code** to verify
4. This just proves you're a real person

### Step 2: Confirm Android Device

**Options:**

**Option A: Just Say "Yes"**
- Even if you don't have a physical device
- You can use the emulator you have
- Or say you'll get one later
- This is just a confirmation checkbox

**Option B: Skip Device Verification**
- Some screens have a "Skip" or "Do this later" option
- Look for that if available

**Option C: Use Your Emulator**
- Your emulator counts as having an Android device
- You can say "Yes, I have a device"
- Even if the verification doesn't work, the checkbox should

### Step 3: After Verification

Once you complete this step, you should be able to:
- Skip internal testing setup
- Go straight to Production when ready
- Upload your `.aab` file directly
