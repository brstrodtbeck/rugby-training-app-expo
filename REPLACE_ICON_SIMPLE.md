# Simple: Replace Your App Icons

## Yes, Those Are The Right Files!

In your `assets` folder, you need to replace:

1. **`icon.png`** ← Replace this (iOS app icon)
2. **`adaptive-icon.png`** ← Replace this (Android app icon)

## Simple Steps

1. **Get your new icon** (1024x1024px PNG)

2. **Replace `icon.png`:**
   - Delete the old `icon.png` file
   - Save your new icon as `icon.png` in the `assets` folder

3. **Replace `adaptive-icon.png`:**
   - Delete the old `adaptive-icon.png` file  
   - Save your new icon as `adaptive-icon.png` in the `assets` folder

4. **Rebuild your apps** (to include new icons)

5. **Resubmit to stores** (with new builds)

---

**That's it!** Just replace those two files with your new icon.
