# Android Studio Quick Facts

## What is Android Studio?

**Android Studio** = Development tool that **includes** the Android emulator

Think of it like this:
- **Android Studio** = The whole package (like a toolbox)
- **Android Emulator** = One tool inside that package (like a specific wrench)

You download Android Studio, and the emulator comes with it!

## What You'll Get

When you download Android Studio, you get:
- ✅ Android Studio IDE (the main program)
- ✅ Android Emulator (runs Android on your computer)
- ✅ Android SDK (development tools)
- ✅ All necessary components

## Is It Free?

**Yes!** Android Studio is completely free - no cost, no subscription.

## What It Looks Like

- **Android Studio window** = Your main workspace
- **Emulator window** = Looks like an Android phone on your screen
- You can run both at the same time

## System Requirements

**Minimum:**
- Windows 10 or 11
- 8GB RAM
- 10GB free disk space
- Internet connection (for downloads)

**Recommended:**
- 16GB RAM
- SSD storage (faster)

## Time Investment

- **First-time setup:** 1-2 hours (mostly downloading)
- **After that:** Emulator starts in 1-2 minutes
- **Worth it:** Yes! You can test Android apps anytime

## Alternative Options

If Android Studio seems too heavy:

1. **Online Android Emulators** (simpler but limited):
   - BrowserStack
   - Genymotion Cloud
   - (Most are paid, limited free tiers)

2. **Borrow a device** (temporary solution)

3. **Skip testing** and submit directly (not recommended)

---

**Bottom Line:** Android Studio is the official, free way to run Android emulator on your computer. It's worth the setup time!
