# Android Studio Welcome Screen - Next Steps

## You're in the Right Place! ✅

Looking at your screen, you can see:

1. **"More Actions" dropdown** is open (below the main buttons)
2. In that dropdown, you'll see: **"Virtual Device Manager"** (with a smartphone icon)

## What to Click

**Click on "Virtual Device Manager"** in the "More Actions" dropdown menu.

This will open the Device Manager where you can:
- Create a new Android emulator
- Download system images
- Start/stop emulators

## After Clicking "Virtual Device Manager"

You'll see a new window with:

1. **A list of virtual devices** (probably empty at first)
2. **A "Create Device" button** (this is what you want!)

## Next Steps After Opening Device Manager

1. Click **"Create Device"** button
2. Choose a device (e.g., Pixel 5 or Pixel 6)
3. Download a system image (e.g., Android 13)
4. Finish setup
5. Start the emulator!

---

**Action:** Click **"Virtual Device Manager"** in the "More Actions" dropdown that's already open on your screen!
