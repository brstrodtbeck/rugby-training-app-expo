# Device Setup - What to Do Next

## What You See

You're in the "Configure virtual device" dialog:
- ✅ Device: **Pixel 9** (already selected)
- ✅ System Image: **Google Play Intel x86_64 Atom System Image** (highlighted in blue)
- ✅ Shows: **"1.8 GB system image will be downloaded"**

## What to Do

**Click "Next" button** (bottom right of the dialog)

This will:
1. Start downloading the system image (1.8 GB)
2. Set up your virtual device
3. Let you finish the configuration

## What Happens Next

After clicking "Next":

1. **Download starts:**
   - The 1.8 GB system image will download
   - This takes 10-20 minutes (depends on internet speed)
   - You'll see a progress bar

2. **After download:**
   - You'll see a "Finish" or "Complete" screen
   - Click "Finish" to complete setup

3. **Device is ready:**
   - Your Pixel 9 emulator will appear in Device Manager
   - You can start it anytime!

## Notes

- The system image selected (API 36.0) is fine - it's a recent Android version
- "Google Play" means you'll have access to Google Play Store in the emulator
- The download is large but only happens once

---

**Action:** Click **"Next"** button to proceed with the download and setup!
