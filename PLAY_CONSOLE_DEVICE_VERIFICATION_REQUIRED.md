# Google Play Console Device Verification - Required for New Accounts

## The Situation

Google Play Console **requires** new developer accounts (created in 2024+) to verify they have access to a real Android device. This is **mandatory** and cannot be skipped.

## Why You're Blocked

This verification:
- Requires the **Play Console mobile app** (not just any Android device)
- Must be done on a **real Android device** (emulators don't work for this)
- Blocks you from releasing apps until completed

## Your Options

**Best solution for immediate progress:**

1. **Ask a friend/family member** if you can borrow their Android phone for 5 minutes

4. **Complete verification** (takes 2 minutes)
5. **Return the device**

**Pros:** Free, fast, you're unblocked immediately

## How to Complete Verification (When You Have a Device)

1. On your Android device, **install "Play Console" app** from Google Play Store
2. On your computer, go to Play Console → Home page
3. Find the verification task, click "View details"
4. **Scan the QR code** with your Android device (Play Console app will open)
5. **Log in** with your Google account (brstrodtbeck@gmail.com)
6. **Tap "Verify"** in the app
7. Done! (Takes 2 minutes)
