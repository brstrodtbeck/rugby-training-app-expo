## What You Need

### Google Play Store Screenshots

**Required:**
- At least **2 screenshots** (up to 8)
- **Recommended sizes:**
  - Phone: **1080 x 1920px** (16:9) or **1080 x 2340px** (19.5:9)
  - Tablet: **1200 x 1920px** (optional)
- **Feature graphic:** 1024 x 500px (required)

### iOS App Store Screenshots

**Required:**
- At least **3 screenshots** (up to 10)
- **Required sizes for iPhone:**
  - 6.7" display (iPhone 14 Pro Max, etc.): **1290 x 2796px**
  - 6.5" display (iPhone 11 Pro Max, etc.): **1242 x 2688px**
  - 5.5" display (iPhone 8 Plus, etc.): **1242 x 2208px**
- **iPad screenshots** (optional but recommended)

## What Screenshots to Take

Show your app's best features:

1. **Home Screen** - Main dashboard
2. **Workout Screen** - Exercise/workout view
3. **Progress/Statistics** - Charts, analytics
4. **Readiness Score** - If you have a nice UI for this
5. **Exercise History** - Show tracking features
6. **Program Selection** - Show available programs

**Recommended:** 4-6 good screenshots showing different features

## How to Take Screenshots

### On Android Emulator

1. **Start your emulator** (you already have it set up!)
2. **Open your app** in the emulator
3. **Navigate to screens** you want to screenshot
4. **Take screenshot:**
   - Click the camera icon in the emulator toolbar
   - Or press `Ctrl + S` (Windows)
   - Or use the screenshot button in emulator controls
5. **Save screenshots** to your computer

### On iOS Device (TestFlight)

1. **Open your app** via TestFlight (when ready)
2. **Navigate to screens** you want to screenshot
3. **Take screenshot:**
   - iPhone X and later: Press Volume Up + Side Button
   - iPhone 8 and earlier: Press Home + Side Button
4. **Screenshots saved** to Photos app
5. **Transfer to computer** via iCloud, email, or USB

## Resize Screenshots (If Needed)

If your screenshots aren't the exact size, you can resize them:

**Free Tools:**
- **Online:** https://www.iloveimg.com/resize-image
- **Windows:** Paint 3D or Photos app
- **Mac:** Preview

**Quick Resize Guide:**
- Google Play: Resize to 1080px width, maintain aspect ratio
- iOS: Use exact dimensions listed above
