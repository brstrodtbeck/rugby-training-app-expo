## Step 1: Create Privacy Policy (REQUIRED) ⚠️

**Status:** 🟡 Not Started  
**Time:** 15-30 minutes  
**Priority:** HIGH - Required for both stores

### Why You Need This

Both Google Play and Apple App Store **require** a privacy policy URL before you can submit your app.

### What You Need

- Privacy policy explaining what data your app collects
- Hosted on HTTPS URL (must be accessible online)
- Covers: data collection, usage, user rights

I'll help you create one customized for your app. Let me prepare that for you...

## Step 2: Prepare Screenshots

**Status:** 🟡 Not Started  
**Time:** 1-2 hours  
**Priority:** HIGH - Required for store listings

### What You Need

- Android: 2-8 screenshots
- iOS: 3+ screenshots (various sizes)
- Feature graphic for Android (1024x500px)

---

## Step 3: Write App Descriptions

**Status:** 🟡 Not Started  
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Priority:** MEDIUM - Required for listings

### What You Need

- Full description (up to 4000 characters)
- Short description for Android (80 characters)
- Keywords for iOS (100 characters)

---

## Step 4: Complete Store Listings

**Status:** 🟡 Not Started  
**Time:** 1-2 hours  
**Priority:** HIGH - Required before submission

### What You Need

- Upload screenshots
- Add descriptions
- Add privacy policy URL
- Complete rating questionnaires
- Support URL (iOS)

---

## Step 5: Submit to Google Play Store

**Status:** 🟡 Not Started  
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Priority:** HIGH

### Steps

1. Upload `.aab` file to Production
2. Complete store listing
3. Submit for review
4. Wait 1-3 days

---

## Step 6: Submit to iOS App Store

**Status:** 🟡 Not Started  
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Priority:** HIGH

### Steps

1. Complete store listing in App Store Connect
2. Submit for review
3. Wait 1-7 days
