# Copyright Information Setup for App Store Connect

## Quick Answer

The copyright field in App Store Connect requires:
- **Format:** `© [Year] [Your Name or Company Name]`
- **Location:** App Store Connect → Your App → App Information → Copyright field

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Go to App Store Connect

1. Visit: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. Sign in with your Apple ID: `brstrodtbeck@gmail.com`
3. Click **"My Apps"**
4. Select **"Progressive Overlord"** (App ID: 6756135098)

### 2. Navigate to App Information

1. In the left sidebar, click **"App Information"**
2. Scroll down to find the **"Copyright"** field

### 3. Enter Your Copyright Information

**Recommended Format:**

```
© 2025 BRIAN RUSSEL STRODTBECK
```

Or if you prefer using your full name:

```

```

**Options:**

- **Individual Developer:** `© 2025 [Your Full Name]`
- **Business/Company:** `© 2025 [Your Company Name]`

- **Team:** BRIAN RUSSEL STRODTBECK (Individual)
- **Team ID:** DX22QJ8ADV

## What the Copyright Statement Means

The copyright statement:
- Shows who owns the app
- Protects your intellectual property
- Required by Apple for app submission
- Appears in the App Store listing

## Current Year

Since we're in 2025, use **2025** as the year. If you plan to update the app in future years, you can include a range like:

```
© 2025-2026 BRIAN RUSSEL STRODTBECK
```

But for the first submission, just the current year is fine:

```
© 2025 BRIAN RUSSEL STRODTBECK
```
