## Using the App

### Starting a Training Session

1. From the **Home Screen**, tap on a scheduled session
2. **Readiness Check**: When the session opens, you'll be asked to rate your readiness (1-10)
   - **8-10 (High)**: Feeling great! Weight will increase by 2-5%, may add an extra set
   - **4-7 (Moderate)**: Standard workout as programmed
   - **1-3 (Low)**: Recovery mode - weight reduced by 5-10%, one less set
   - You can skip this and use the default (moderate)
3. Review the exercises for today's session

### Logging Your Workout

For each exercise:

1. **Review Suggested Weight**: The app shows a suggested weight based on:
   - Your previous performance
   - The Rule of 3 progression (see below)
   - Your readiness score

2. **Enter Your Actual Performance**:
   - Tap each set to log weight and reps
   - Enter the weight you actually lifted
   - Enter the reps you completed
   - Optionally enter RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) - how hard it felt

3. **Rest Timer**: 
   - Tap "Start Rest" between sets
   - Timer counts down automatically
   - Helps maintain proper rest periods

4. **Complete Exercises**: 
   - Check off each exercise as you finish
   - Progress is automatically saved

5. **Finish Session**: 
   - Review your session summary
   - See progression suggestions for next time

## Weight and Progression Logic

The app uses intelligent progression to ensure you're always training at the right intensity. Here's how it works:

### The Rule of 3 System

The core progression logic is called the **"Rule of 3"**:

**Basic Rule**: After **3 consecutive successful sessions** completing all sets and reps with good form, the app will suggest increasing weight.

**What Counts as a Success?**
- Completed all prescribed sets and reps
- Maintained proper form (quality gate)
- RPE (perceived effort) was within target range

**Weight Increases:**
- **Compound exercises** (squats, deadlifts, presses): 2.5-5% increase (typically 2.5-5 lbs)
- **Assistance exercises** (rows, curls, etc.): 2.5-5% increase (typically 2.5-5 lbs)
- Increases are rounded to nearest 2.5 or 5 lbs for practical gym use

**Example:**
- Session 1: Bench Press 135 lbs × 3 sets × 5 reps ✅
- Session 2: Bench Press 135 lbs × 3 sets × 5 reps ✅
- Session 3: Bench Press 135 lbs × 3 sets × 5 reps ✅
- **Session 4: App suggests 140 lbs** (Rule of 3 triggered!)

### Extra Reps Fallback

If you complete **3 or more extra reps** on your last set in a single session, the app will suggest a weight increase immediately - you don't have to wait for 3 consecutive sessions.

**Example:**
- Prescribed: 3 sets × 10 reps
- You complete: 10, 10, **13 reps** (3 extra on last set)
- **Next session: Weight increase suggested** (even if it's only your first success)

### Readiness Safeguards

The app monitors your readiness scores to prevent overtraining:

**Low Readiness Hold:**
- If you have **one session** with low readiness (1-3) but still complete your workout → Weight stays the same (no progression, but no reduction)
- This prevents unnecessary weight drops from single bad days

**Low Readiness Reduction:**
- If you have **two consecutive sessions** with low readiness (1-3) AND you miss reps → Weight reduced by 5%
- This protects you from training too heavy when you're not recovered

**Example:**
- Session 1: Readiness 2, missed reps → No reduction yet (waiting to see if it's a pattern)
- Session 2: Readiness 3, missed reps again → **Weight reduced by 5%** (two consecutive low readiness + failures)

### Deload Weeks

During programmed deload weeks:

- **All progression is paused** - even if you hit 3 consecutive successes
- Weight is typically reduced to 85% of working weight
- After deload week ends, you return to your pre-deload weight (not the deload weight)
- The app remembers where you were and resumes from there

### Quality Gates

Before the app suggests progression, it checks:

- **Form quality**: Were you able to maintain proper tempo and range of motion?
- **RPE consistency**: Was your perceived effort in the target range (±1 RPE)?
- **Completion**: Did you finish all sets and reps as prescribed?

Only sessions that pass all quality gates count toward your "Rule of 3" streak.

### Weight Calculation Details

**Starting Weight:**
- Based on your 1RM values entered during setup
- Calculated as a percentage of your max (e.g., 85% for strength work)

**Progressions:**
- Minimum increment: 2.5 lbs (for smaller exercises)
- Maximum increment: 5 lbs (for larger compound movements)
- All increments rounded to nearest standard plate size

**Weight Reduction:**
- Typically 5% when triggered by readiness safeguards
- Rounded to nearest practical weight for gym equipment

## Readiness Scoring

### How to Rate Your Readiness

Before each workout, rate how you feel on a scale of 1-10:

**1-3 (Low Readiness):**
- Feeling tired, sore, or unmotivated
- Poor sleep or recovery
- Stress or fatigue from work/life
- **Result**: Weight reduced 5-10%, one less set

**4-7 (Moderate Readiness):**
- Normal energy levels
- Ready for standard workout
- **Result**: Standard prescription, no changes

**8-10 (High Readiness):**
- Feeling strong and energized
- Well recovered
- Ready to push harder
- **Result**: Weight increased 2-5%, may add one set

### When to Skip Readiness

You can skip the readiness check - the app will default to moderate (5), which means no adjustments to your workout.

## Updating Your 1RM

Your 1RM (One Rep Max) values are used to calculate starting weights and track progress:

1. Go to **"My 1RMs"** from the Home screen

**When to Update:**
- After testing your max
- When you feel your strength has increased significantly
- The app will also suggest updates based on your logged workouts

## Troubleshooting

### Weight Suggestions Seem Wrong

- Check your 1RM values are current in "My 1RMs"
- Review your exercise history - the app bases suggestions on your last few sessions
- Remember: suggestions are just that - you can adjust based on how you feel

### Progression Not Triggering

The Rule of 3 requires:
- 3 consecutive successful sessions
- All sets and reps completed
- Quality gates passed (form, RPE)
- Not in a deload week

Check your exercise history to see your streak count.

### Readiness Score Not Affecting Workout

Readiness adjustments only apply to exercises that use the hybrid 1RM system. Some exercises in your program may use different progression methods.

### App Not Saving Progress

- Ensure you're connected to the internet (for initial sync)
- All data is stored locally on your device
- Progress auto-saves as you log sets
- If issues persist, try restarting the app

## Quick Reference

### Progression Triggers
- ✅ **3 consecutive successes** → Weight increase
- ⚡ **+3 extra reps in one session** → Immediate weight increase
- ⚠️ **2 consecutive low readiness + missed reps** → Weight decrease (5%)
- ⏸️ **Deload week** → All progression paused

### Weight Changes
- **Increase**: 2.5-5% (2.5-5 lbs typically)
- **Decrease**: 5% when triggered by safeguards
- **High Readiness**: +2-5% for that session only
- **Low Readiness**: -5-10% for that session only

### Readiness Impact
- **High (8-10)**: +2-5% weight, +1 set
- **Moderate (4-7)**: No changes
- **Low (1-3)**: -5-10% weight, -1 set
