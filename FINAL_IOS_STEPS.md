# Final Steps to Submit iOS App

## ✅ Completed

- ✅ Screenshots
- ✅ Description
- ✅ Support email

## ⏳ Need to Complete

### 1. Privacy Policy URL

**Where to add:**
- App Store Connect → Your App → App Information
- Look for "Privacy Policy URL" field
- Add your privacy policy URL (the one from the generator)

**Required!** Both stores require this.

### 2. Keywords

**Where to add:**
- App Store Connect → Your App → App Information → Keywords
- Add: `fitness,workout,strength training,rugby,athletics,progression,1rm,weightlifting,training,conditioning`

Or copy from: `IOS_APP_DESCRIPTION.md`

### 3. Age Rating Questionnaire

**Where to add:**
- App Store Connect → Your App → App Information → Age Rating
- Click "Edit" or "Complete Rating"
- Answer questions about your app's content
- Apple will assign a rating

**Questions typically ask about:**
- Violence
- Sexual content
- Gambling
- Drugs/alcohol
- Medical/treatment information
- Unrestricted web access
- etc.

For a fitness app, answers are usually:
- Mostly "No" to most questions
- "Infrequent/Mild" if any content applies

---

## After Everything is Complete

1. **Check all required fields are filled** (no red errors)
2. **Go to your app version**
3. **Click "Submit for Review"**
4. **Wait for review** (1-7 days typically)
